## The stopping condition

```
RuntimeError: step 39: cumulative optimizer-token mismatch 5.7856% exceeds 5.0000%
```

Raised at `verl/utils/hindsight.py:793`. This is preregistered runtime validity
gate 7 (`E2C_PREREG.md`, "Runtime validity gates"):

> 7. cumulative total optimizer response-token mismatch is at most 5%.
>
> Any violation stops the affected seed before its optimizer update and makes
> the three-seed direction test inconclusive. The source budget, reservoir,
> matcher, or gates are not changed under the E2c label.

The gate fired **before** the step-39 optimizer update, as specified. The run
did not corrupt any comparator, the reservoir, or any completed artifact.

## The drift was structural, not a spike

Cumulative optimizer-token mismatch by step:

| step | 1 | 9 | 17 | 21 | 25 | 29 | 33 | 37 | 38 | 39 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mismatch | 0% | .017% | .118% | .320% | .453% | 1.435% | 2.347% | 3.399% | 4.673% | **5.786%** |

The series is monotone and accelerating from roughly step 25 onward. It is not
a transient, and no single step is responsible.

### Mechanism

The accounting identifies the cause, and it is a property of the
immutable-reservoir design rather than an implementation defect:

- `fallback_slots` is **0 at every one of the 38 steps**, so validity gate 2
  held throughout. The matcher never failed to find a source; it was not
  starved in the sense the protocol guards against.
- What degrades instead is **source diversity**. In the last ten recorded
  steps the matcher drew on a single distinct reservoir source on six of them,
  serving as many as seven replacement groups in one step from that one entry.
  Early steps used three to four distinct sources per step.
- `dead_slots` sits at 5–8 per step late in training, versus 2–4 early, so
  progressively more slots require replacement at exactly the point when the
  usable informative-source pool has narrowed.
- Because one fixed source supplies many groups, one fixed response length is
  substituted for targets of varying length. Actual optimizer response tokens
  reach 6,823 against a 4,481 target at step 38.

In short: **as the policy sharpens, more groups die, and the frozen reservoir
cannot supply enough distinct length-compatible informative sources to keep
dose-matching inside the preregistered 5% tolerance.** The immutable-reservoir
constraint and the dose-matching constraint become jointly unsatisfiable late
in a 60-step run.

## Consequences, per the frozen rule

1. **The three-seed direction test is inconclusive.** It is not negative and
   not positive. No directional claim about failure recycling may be drawn from
   E2c.
2. **Nothing is retried, and no gate, budget, reservoir, or matcher is
   changed under the E2c label.** The preregistration forbids it explicitly.
   The partial `replay_accounting.jsonl` and the partial training log are
   retained as the evidence of the stop and must not be deleted.
3. **The paper ships on the basis already written into `body_iclr.tex`:**
   "until that protocol completes, we report the logged proxy under its actual
   provenance." That sentence stands unchanged and now has a dated closure
   behind it.
4. **The Aug 28 training stop is satisfied early.** The RTX 5090 is released
   for other project work from 2026-08-15 00:12 EDT.

## What this is worth scientifically

E2c produced no endpoint, but it is not a null event. It converts a design
assumption into a measured boundary:

> Dose-matched replay from a frozen, immutable reservoir degrades as the policy
> sharpens, because the pool of distinct informative sources that are also
> length-compatible shrinks exactly when the demand for replacements grows.

This is reportable as a limitation of the replay design under its own
preregistered tolerance, with the drift series above as evidence. It is
stronger than "the protocol did not run," and it is honest: the gate was
written before the data and it did its job.

It also lowers the value of any future attempt at the same contrast without a
design change. A replacement study would need a different source policy (for
example a refreshed or larger reservoir, or length-aware matching), and would
have to be preregistered and labelled separately from E2c.
