# 32x1 result: accumulation does not rescue the pure activity score

Development sweep, 15 runs, 3 arms x 5 seeds x 5,000 updates, upstream configs
verbatim except the score. **Development only — not evidence.**

## The question

`DIAGNOSIS_2026-08-16.md` located the earlier loss in the 4x8 restructuring
(−0.207 for MaxMC too) rather than in the score, and asked whether the PLR
buffer's accumulated per-level posterior could supply Bernoulli fidelity over
revisits *without* paying the diversity cost. So: run coefficient activity at
full 32x1 diversity, directly comparable to the winning upstream arm.

## Answer: no

Held-out return, mean over the three test mazes:

| arm | mean | sd | vs plrMM32 (paired) | pairs | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **plrMM32 — upstream robust PLR** | **0.6288** | 0.091 | — | — | — |
| plrCA32N16 | 0.5510 | 0.087 | −0.078 | 1/5 | 0.19 |
| drControl — no curriculum | 0.5390 | 0.168 | −0.090 | 2/5 | 0.50 |
| plrCA32N8 | 0.5004 | 0.090 | **−0.128** | **0/5** | **0.0625** |
| accelCA32N8 | 0.4009 | 0.138 | −0.228 | 1/5 | 0.125 |

Three findings:

1. **Full diversity does not help the pure activity score.** 32x1 vs 4x8 with
   the same score is +0.019 (3/5, p=.88) in PLR and −0.100 (2/5) in ACCEL —
   noise. Whatever the 4x8 restructuring cost, removing it did not recover the
   score's deficit.
2. **Pure activity at N=8 loses to upstream MaxMC in every seed** (0/5,
   p=.0625, the minimum for five seeds), by −0.128. It also fails to beat the
   no-curriculum control.
3. **N=16 is the best pure-activity arm and the only one near the control**,
   +0.051 over N=8 at 32x1 (3/5, p=.38). Weak, but it is the second time N=16
   has looked better than N=8 at 32x1 — and note it is now the *harder* peak
   that helps, consistent with the Acrobot dose–response, whereas the earlier
   4x8 pilot had N=8 ahead. Neither ordering is significant.

## Interpretation

The information-deficit hypothesis in `MATH_REVIEW_2026-08-16.md` §3 survives
this test. Accumulating one bit per visit over revisits does not make a
success-rate score competitive with a regret proxy that reads the critic at
every timestep. Configuration (a) — replacing MaxMC with `u_N(p̂)` — is now
tested at 4x8 and at 32x1, in PLR and in ACCEL, at N=8 and N=16, across 5
seeds each, and never beats upstream robust PLR. **Configuration (a) is
closed.** Its ceiling on AMaze at this budget is roughly the no-curriculum
control.

That is a real result, and it parallels Acrobot exactly: the *shape* of `u_N`
is a defensible difficulty target (the Acrobot dose–response), but as a
*replacement* for a richer signal it is starved.

## What is still open

**Configuration (b), activity-gated MaxMC** — `score = max(MaxMC,0) · u_N(p̂)`
— keeps the per-timestep signal and uses activity only to suppress mastered
and hopeless levels. It addresses the deficit rather than paying for it, and it
is the last cheap variant. **Running now** (overlay v6): gate N=8, gate+decay
0.7, gate in ACCEL, 5 seeds each.

**Configuration (c), the MaxRL-estimator student**, is the faithful mapping and
remains a separate, clearly-labelled study.

## Rule for reading the gate result

`plrMM32` = 0.6288 is the bar. If gating does not at least match it, the
honest lane conclusion is a boundary — coefficient activity does not improve
regret-based replay on AMaze at 5,000 updates — and that boundary is reported,
not tuned away. If gating matches or beats it, *that* arm is preregistered for
a confirmatory campaign at full budget with fresh seeds; nothing from these
development sweeps enters the paper as evidence.
