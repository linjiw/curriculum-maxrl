## Contract Paraphrase

**D1 — methodology_rigor.** I will assess whether the research design, experimental unit, sampling and pairing structure, data-handling rules, statistical analyses, uncertainty reporting, and reproducibility materials are adequate for the claims being made. For quantitative ML experiments, the decisive issue is whether inference is based on genuinely independent units such as seeds or warm-start blocks rather than correlated rollouts or repeated evaluations, with prospective analysis rules, transparent treatment of failed or missing runs, appropriate effect estimates and intervals, and enough procedural detail to reproduce the computation. This is a mandatory dimension.

**D2 — domain_accuracy.** I will assess whether the paper uses reinforcement-learning, curriculum-selection, estimator, and verifiable-reward concepts accurately; states mathematical and empirical claims with their required assumptions; compares methods and metrics on commensurate terms; and represents relevant prior results without factual distortion. From a methodology perspective, domain accuracy also requires that any implementation claimed to instantiate a theoretical object actually matches its definition and that numerical claims can be checked against declared derivations or artifacts. This is a mandatory dimension.

**D3 — argumentative_coherence.** I will assess whether the central thesis follows from the stated premises, formal results, and empirical design, and whether the strength of each conclusion matches the evidence tier that supports it. I will look especially for invalid transitions from coefficient-level or diagnostic quantities to learning utility, from association to causation, from a selected configuration to a general law, or from exploratory evidence to confirmatory claims, as well as contradictions among hypotheses, methods, results, and limitations. This is a mandatory dimension.

**D4 — cross_disciplinary_relevance.** I will assess whether definitions, units of analysis, estimands, uncertainty statements, and claimed implications are intelligible to readers in adjacent areas of machine learning, statistics, and reinforcement learning, and whether any transfer beyond the immediate setting is supported rather than asserted. Methodological boundaries should make clear which conclusions depend on binary rewards, group construction, independence assumptions, estimator normalization, or a particular computational substrate. This is a high-priority dimension.

**D5 — writing_and_structure.** I will assess whether the organization and exposition let a reviewer trace research questions to assumptions, methods, analyses, results, and limitations; whether figures and tables identify units, sample sizes, uncertainty, and aggregation; and whether the manuscript follows ICLR conventions closely enough for its claims to be audited. Statistical notation, labels, appendix routing, and reproducibility pointers should be consistent and unambiguous. This is a normal-priority dimension.

## Scoring Plan

### D1: methodology_rigor

**dimension_id:** D1

**what_to_look_for:** I will identify the inferential target and experimental unit for every load-bearing empirical claim; verify that independent seeds, tasks, environments, checkpoints, rollout groups, and repeated evaluations are distinguished; and check that paired or blocked designs are analyzed at the level at which randomization or independent replication occurred. I will inspect whether hypotheses, endpoints, stopping rules, exclusions, missing-cell rules, and outcome branches were set prospectively where confirmatory language is used; whether deviations and failed runs remain visible; and whether treatment delivery and implementation fidelity are measured independently of the endpoint. I will check sample-size justification through prospective power, randomization-based operating characteristics, or sensitivity/minimum-detectable-effect analysis; require effect estimates, clearly named uncertainty intervals, exact test procedures, and practical as well as statistical interpretation; and examine multiplicity, distributional assumptions, bootstrap resampling units, Monte Carlo error, and robustness analyses. For theoretical or machine-verified components, I will look for explicit assumptions, derivations, edge cases, counterexamples, and tests against the deployed implementation. Reproducibility signals include pinned environments, immutable inputs, seeds, source and artifact hashes, complete run matrices, executable analyzers, data provenance, and a documented path from raw outcomes to reported numbers and figures.

**what_triggers_block:** A block is triggered if a central empirical conclusion is not identified by the design or is statistically unsupported—for example, correlated rollouts or evaluation points are treated as independent replicates; pairing or randomization is broken or ignored in a way that changes the inferential result; endpoints, samples, seeds, exclusions, or stopping rules were adaptively selected after outcome inspection without a valid correction and transparent exploratory labeling; missing or failed runs are selectively omitted; a causal claim lacks a controlled intervention on the named treatment; or the reported test/interval is incompatible with the sampling design. It is also triggered if a central theorem or computational claim depends on unstated or violated assumptions, if the implementation cannot plausibly realize the described method, or if absent provenance/code/data makes the load-bearing result impossible to audit even in principle. The pattern must threaten the validity of the main methodological conclusion, not merely reflect a formatting omission.

**what_triggers_warn:** A warn is triggered when the core design remains interpretable but important rigor is incomplete or localized—for example, too few independent seeds without an adequate prospective power or sensitivity argument; imprecise intervals not discussed as Type II risk; effect sizes, descriptive summaries, exact sample counts, or interval definitions omitted for some key comparisons; bootstrap or randomization procedures insufficiently specify the resampling/exchangeability unit; reasonable assumptions are not diagnosed; multiple secondary analyses lack a stated correction or exploratory label; missingness and engineering failures are incompletely documented; or reproducibility depends on avoidable environment details, undocumented preprocessing, or partially unavailable artifacts. Minor APA-style issues alone will not escalate beyond warn unless they conceal the underlying analysis.

### D2: domain_accuracy

**dimension_id:** D2

**what_to_look_for:** I will verify the definitions and operationalizations of estimators, coefficient or activity measures, success-count laws, curriculum units, rewards, pass-rate quantities, and learning-utility endpoints. I will check equations algebraically where feasible, confirm boundary cases and normalization conventions, and compare stated theoretical objects with the coefficients and sufficient statistics actually implemented. Empirical comparisons should hold the claimed controls fixed, use the same budget semantics and evaluation metric, distinguish standard estimands from proxies, and avoid comparing magnitudes whose scale changes with estimator normalization or optimizer settings. I will also check whether citations and descriptions of prior methods support the specific methodological distinctions and novelty claims attributed to them.

**what_triggers_block:** A block is triggered if the central contribution relies on a materially false domain statement: an incorrect estimator formula or theorem, a conflation of mean pass rate with a count distribution where that distinction controls the result, a mismatch between the named estimator and deployed implementation, a noncommensurate metric or budget presented as a fair comparison, or a factual mischaracterization of prior work that invalidates the novelty claim. It is likewise triggered if key numerical results use mislabeled units or estimands such that the substantive conclusion changes under the correct definition.

**what_triggers_warn:** A warn is triggered by domain imprecision that does not overturn the central result but could mislead readers—for example, assumptions or normalization dependence stated only implicitly, ambiguous use of task/instance/group terminology, proxy metrics insufficiently distinguished from standard metrics, incomplete discussion of estimator-specific scaling, or a localized overstatement of what an implementation check establishes. A correct main claim with incomplete positioning of a closely related prior method also triggers warn unless the omission directly collapses the claimed contribution.

### D3: argumentative_coherence

**dimension_id:** D3

**what_to_look_for:** I will construct a claim-to-evidence map linking the thesis and each contribution to a proof, preregistered test, controlled descriptive analysis, or explicitly open question. I will check whether theorem assumptions match the prose scope; whether diagnostic, mechanistic, causal, predictive, and utility claims are kept distinct; whether negative, null, mixed, and failed-delivery outcomes are reported at their earned strength; and whether alternative explanations and counterexamples are addressed. I will examine whether conclusions are invariant to the stated unit of analysis and whether evidence from one estimator, environment, group size, or selected hyperparameter is generalized only with an explicit warrant.

**what_triggers_block:** A block is triggered when a central inference does not follow from its evidence—for example, algebraic activity is treated as proof of optimization benefit; observational or post-selected patterns are presented as causal or confirmatory; a theorem proved under a restricted stochastic model is claimed for arbitrary dependence; an intervention changes multiple factors while attributing the endpoint to one; or contradictory primary outcomes are omitted or reinterpreted so that the thesis becomes unfalsifiable. It is also triggered by a central logical inconsistency among the formal statement, empirical estimand, and conclusion.

**what_triggers_warn:** A warn is triggered when the main argument remains supportable but some claims exceed their evidence locally, assumptions are separated too far from the statements they qualify, alternative explanations are acknowledged without an adequate discriminating analysis, or confirmatory and exploratory results are not consistently labeled. Overgeneralization from limited environments, seeds, estimators, reward types, or hyperparameter sweeps triggers warn when the manuscript otherwise preserves the narrower valid conclusion.

### D4: cross_disciplinary_relevance

**dimension_id:** D4

**what_to_look_for:** I will check whether a reader outside the immediate subarea can identify the data-generating unit, estimator-controlled quantity, curriculum decision, causal contrast, and learning endpoint without relying on local jargon. Definitions should state the relationship among task-level outcomes, group-level count laws, rollout dependence, and training utility; examples should expose rather than hide limiting assumptions. I will look for calibrated implications to adjacent areas, explicit scope conditions, and enough statistical explanation for readers to understand paired designs, randomization inference, bootstrap intervals, preregistration status, and the distinction between computational verification and empirical confirmation.

**what_triggers_block:** A block is triggered if the paper's high-priority relevance claim systematically rests on an invalid transfer across units, estimators, reward types, or research paradigms, or if core constructs are defined so opaquely or inconsistently that adjacent-field readers would infer a materially different estimand. Broad interdisciplinary prescriptions unsupported by the design also trigger block when they are central rather than incidental.

**what_triggers_warn:** A warn is triggered when the immediate technical result is sound but accessibility or transfer is underdeveloped: essential jargon lacks a plain-language definition, the statistical design is not explained outside field shorthand, examples do not clarify the unit-of-analysis distinction, implications are stated beyond the evaluated settings without a clear boundary, or limitations needed to prevent cross-field misapplication are present only in an appendix.

### D5: writing_and_structure

**dimension_id:** D5

**what_to_look_for:** I will trace whether the title, abstract, introduction, theory, methods, results, limitations, conclusion, appendix, and reproducibility materials use consistent claims and terminology. I will inspect whether every theorem is followed by an interpretation, every empirical result identifies the independent sample size and uncertainty type, and every figure/table declares aggregation, axes, units, baselines, and data provenance. I will check that confirmatory and exploratory material is visually and verbally distinguishable; that all quantitative claims route to tables, figures, or artifacts; that statistical symbols, exact p-values, intervals, and effect-size descriptions are consistently formatted; and that the approximately 12,000-word manuscript uses appendices without hiding information necessary to assess validity. I will also check adherence to ICLR submission structure, length, anonymity, citation, and supplementary-material conventions.

**what_triggers_block:** A block is triggered if organization or presentation prevents methodological audit—for example, essential design or analysis details are absent rather than merely deferred, figures/tables conceal the replicate or uncertainty unit, contradictory values cannot be reconciled, the main claims cannot be traced to reported evidence, or venue noncompliance makes the submission unreviewable. Because this is a normal-priority dimension, block requires a pervasive structural failure rather than ordinary prose or formatting defects.

**what_triggers_warn:** A warn is triggered by material but repairable presentation problems: inconsistent terminology, unclear theorem-to-experiment transitions, insufficient figure/table captions, missing sample-size or uncertainty labels, awkward separation of assumptions and claims, weak navigation to appendix evidence or artifacts, statistical-format inconsistencies, or venue/anonymity/length issues that can be corrected without redesigning the research. Isolated copyediting issues will be noted only if they affect interpretation.
